Privacy Policy

Last updated: 02/10/2026

1. Information we access

When you connect your TikTok account, and only after you grant permission on TikTok's authorization screen, the App may access:

  • Basic profile information (display name, avatar, open ID)
  • The list of your videos and their statistics (views, likes, comments, shares)
  • The ability to upload or publish videos and photos on your behalf

2. How we use this information

This information is used only to provide the features of the App: publishing and scheduling your content, and displaying statistics about your own videos.

3. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored securely and used only to perform actions you requested. We do not store your TikTok password.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data only as long as necessary to provide the service. You can revoke access at any time from your TikTok settings, and you can ask us to delete any data we hold by contacting us at the address below.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
